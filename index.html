Privacy Policy for Legend DEO

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Legend DEO ("the Extension") is a Chrome extension developed to assist with land-record automation and referral tracking for Bangladeshi land administration workflows. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension and its companion admin panel collect, how that data is used, stored, and shared, and what choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Legend DEO consists of a Chrome Extension and a companion website (registration/renewal pages, leaderboard, and admin panel). Depending on how you use them, we may collect:

  • Registration information (website): your name, email address, and (if you sign up through a referral) a referral/promo code, submitted when you register for a license or a free trial.
  • Payment coordination details (website): the bKash, Nagad, or Rocket mobile wallet number you send payment from, used only to verify and match your payment to your registration. We do not collect wallet PINs or passwords, and we do not process payments ourselves — payments are made directly through bKash/Nagad/Rocket outside the Extension.
  • License and device identifiers (Extension): a randomly generated device ID and your license key, sent to our server only to verify that your subscription is active and to prevent a single license from being used on multiple devices.
  • Installation and browser identifiers (Extension): a randomly generated install ID and basic technical details (browser type, operating system, screen resolution) used to distinguish one installation from another for license verification and to distribute server load. These are not linked to your real-world identity beyond your license/registration record.
  • Land-portal entry data you choose to sync (Extension, "Holding Pro" feature): if you enable the optional holding-entry automation feature, your typed username and the specific correction/entry rows you request are sent to our Google Sheets-based backend so the Extension can fetch your pending tasks and mark them done. Owner/holding data you type directly into government portal forms (e.g., name or address corrections) stays in your browser and is only transmitted when it is part of a task you explicitly queued through this feature.
  • Admin login credentials (admin panel only): the administrator's email address, used to send a one-time password (OTP) for two-factor authentication when accessing the admin panel. This applies only to the site administrator, not regular users.
  • Referral and commission data: referral counts, sub-referral chains, bonus/renewal records, and leaderboard rankings generated from registered users' activity.

We do not knowingly collect national ID numbers, wallet passwords/PINs, or government portal login credentials.

2. How We Collect Information

Information is collected directly through:

  • Registration, trial, and renewal forms on our website (legenddeo.netlify.app).
  • The Extension's license-verification step, which runs automatically when you use it on a supported government portal.
  • The optional holding-entry automation feature, only when you actively enable and use it.

3. How We Store Information

Collected data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database (asia-southeast1 region) and, for the optional holding-entry automation feature, in Google Sheets accessed via Google Apps Script. Firebase access rules and authentication restrict who can read this data. Administrative access to the admin panel is protected by email-based OTP two-factor authentication and an automatic session timeout after inactivity.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Verify that a license is valid, active, and not already bound to another device.
  • Calculate and display referral and commission earnings, and maintain the leaderboard.
  • Verify administrator identity when accessing the admin panel.
  • Match incoming bKash/Nagad/Rocket payments to the correct registration for license activation or renewal.
  • Deliver and track your queued holding-entry automation tasks, if you use that feature.

We do not sell, rent, or use collected data for advertising, and we do not use it for any purpose beyond operating the features described above.

5. How We Share Information

We share limited information with the following third-party service providers, solely to operate the functions described above:

  • Google Firebase — stores license, device, referral, and commission records.
  • Google Sheets / Google Apps Script — stores and serves holding-entry automation task data, only for users who enable that optional feature.
  • EmailJS — delivers OTP emails for admin two-factor authentication. Only the admin's email address and a generated OTP code are sent to EmailJS.
  • Netlify — hosts our website and admin panel files (no personal data is stored by Netlify itself).

We do not share user data with any other third party, and we do not share data for marketing or advertising purposes. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain referral, commission, and account data for as long as necessary to operate the referral and leaderboard system, or until the user requests deletion. Users may request deletion of their data at any time by contacting us using the email address below. Upon request, we will delete the applicable records from our Firebase database within a reasonable time frame.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, including Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA Enterprise) to prevent unauthorized API access, restricted database access rules, and two-factor authentication for administrative accounts. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Extension and admin panel are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
