Privacy Policy — Legend DEO

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Legend DEO ("the Extension", "we", "our") is a Chrome browser extension built to speed up and reduce errors in data-entry work on Bangladesh's land record portals — the Land Development Tax portal (ldtax.gov.bd) and the DLRMS backoffice portal (backoffice.dlrms.land.gov.bd). This policy explains what data the Extension accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and who it is shared with.

1. Summary

  • All holding, khatian, mouja, owner-name, address, NID, phone, and land-share data you enter or copy stays only on your own device, inside your browser's local storage. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this information.
  • The only information transmitted off your device is a license key and an anonymous, randomly generated device identifier, used solely to verify that your license is valid and to enforce single-device use. This is prominently disclosed, with a link to this policy, in the Extension's own license-verification screen before you click "verify."
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. There is no advertising and no analytics/tracking of your browsing activity.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identity information tied to you as a person — only the license key you use and a random device ID.
  • We do not track which websites you visit outside of ldtax.gov.bd.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics/tracking scripts.
  • We do not read, collect, or transmit the content of any other webpage, file, email, or document on your computer.

3. How Data Is Handled

Data you type into the Extension's panels (owner names, addresses, holding numbers, etc.) is written directly to your browser's built-in local storage APIs (chrome.storage.local and window.localStorage). This storage is sandboxed to your browser profile and is not accessible to Legend DEO, to other websites, or to other extensions. It is only read back by the Extension itself to fill forms on the ldtax.gov.bd site on your instruction (e.g., clicking a checkbox or a fill/entry button).

The Extension injects auto-fill values directly into the government portal's own web forms using standard DOM APIs. No holding, owner, or land data is routed through any Legend DEO server in this process.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure) — receives only your license key and an anonymous device UUID, used exclusively for license verification. See Firebase's privacy documentation.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data to any other third party.
  • We do not share data for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data (holding/owner information, settings) remains on your device until you delete it yourself — either through the Extension's own "reset" controls (available for each feature) or by removing the Extension / clearing your browser's extension storage from Chrome's settings.

License-verification records (license key + device UUID) are retained on our license server for as long as the license remains active. You can request deletion of this record at any time using the contact details below.

6. Your Choices

  • Every auto-fill feature in the Extension can be individually turned on or off from its settings panel.
  • You can clear all locally stored holding/owner data at any time using the in-extension "reset" buttons.
  • You can uninstall the Extension at any time from chrome://extensions, which removes its local storage from your browser.

7. Children's Privacy

The Extension is a professional tool intended for land-office data-entry staff and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Extension's features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
